Third-party notices for aare binary packages
============================================

The aare Python wheels and conda packages compile the following libraries into
the aare extension module. They are not part of the aare source repository;
CMake downloads them at build time. Their license notices follow below.

  Minuit2 v6-40-02      LGPL-2.1-or-later  https://github.com/GooFit/Minuit2
  {fmt} 12.1.0          MIT                https://github.com/fmtlib/fmt
  nlohmann/json 3.11.3  MIT                https://github.com/nlohmann/json
  pybind11 3.0.3        BSD-3-Clause       https://github.com/pybind/pybind11

Minuit2 is linked statically. To use aare with a modified Minuit2, build aare
from source (https://github.com/slsdetectorgroup/aare) with
-DAARE_FETCH_MINUIT2=OFF and make your Minuit2 build findable by CMake's
find_package(Minuit2).
